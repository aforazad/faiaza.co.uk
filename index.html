Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to the questions we hear most from customers in the USA and UK.

What comes with a Faiaza necklace order?

Every order includes the necklace, a soft-touch premium gift box, and a heartfelt pre-printed message card specific to the relationship. A luxury box upgrade is also available at checkout on Amazon. It arrives completely gift-ready — no extra wrapping needed.

Where can I buy Faiaza necklaces?

Faiaza necklaces are available on Amazon.com in the USA and Amazon.co.uk in the UK. Both are Fulfilled by Amazon, meaning you get Prime fast delivery and free returns on every order.

Can I send directly to the recipient?

Yes — simply enter the recipient's address at Amazon checkout. The order ships in the gift box with the message card already inside, so it arrives completely ready to open. No trip to the post office required.

What materials are the necklaces made from?

Faiaza necklaces are made from stainless steel with a 14K white gold or 18K yellow gold finish. Each pendant features a premium 6mm cubic zirconia (CZ) stone. The chain adjusts from 18 to 22 inches and fastens with a lobster clasp.

What gold finish options are available?

Most Faiaza necklaces are available in both 14K white gold and 18K yellow gold finish, both plated over stainless steel. Simply select your preferred finish when adding to your Amazon basket.

Are Faiaza necklaces eligible for Amazon Prime?

Yes. Faiaza is Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA) in both the US and UK, so all orders are Prime-eligible. Prime members enjoy fast delivery — often next-day — and free returns are included on every order.

What occasions are the necklaces suited for?

Faiaza necklaces are popular gifts for anniversaries, birthdays, Mother's Day, Valentine's Day, graduations, Christmas, and weddings. Each necklace comes with a message card written for the specific relationship.

What length is the chain?

All Faiaza necklaces come on an adjustable chain that runs from 18 to 22 inches, fastened with a lobster clasp. This suits most necklines and body types.
